# Wired Different

## The ADHD Success Playbook: 12 Systems for Careers, Money, and Focus When Willpower Doesn't Work

---

### Disclaimer

This book is lived-experience and research-informed self-help. It is not medical advice, and nothing in it should replace working with a qualified doctor, psychiatrist, or therapist. Decisions about diagnosis, medication, and treatment belong in that relationship, not in an ebook. What this book offers is the layer clinicians rarely have time for: the practical, day-to-day systems that tend to work for brains like ours.

---

### Introduction: Why Another ADHD Book

Most productivity advice is written for a brain you don't have. You've probably proven that already — the abandoned planners, the eleven-day habit streaks, the todo apps you now feel guilty opening.

This book starts from a different premise: your brain isn't a broken version of a normal one. It's a different operating system, and it needs software compiled for it. Every chapter that follows is one piece of that software — motivation, memory, time, starting, focus, accountability, money, career, emotions, routines — ending with you assembling your own one-page personal operating manual.

You don't have to read it in order. Chapter 1 is the only prerequisite; after that, jump to whatever is on fire this week. That's not a compromise. That's the book working the way your brain does.

Let's get you some compatible software.
