## Description（リード文5ステップ型）

---

**Bought the planner. Used it for eleven days. Found it under the mail a month later?**

If you have ADHD, that's not a discipline problem. It's a compatibility problem: almost all productivity advice is compiled for a brain that runs on *importance*. Yours runs on *interest, novelty, challenge, and urgency* — which is why "just try harder" has failed you for twenty years, and why it will fail for twenty more.

What actually works isn't more willpower. It's **engineering**: changing your environment so the brain you actually have can win. I spent years finding out the hard way — the abandoned projects, the ADHD tax on late fees and forgotten subscriptions, the 2 a.m. hyperfocus followed by three dead weeks.

But "engineer your environment" is easy to say and maddeningly vague to do. So I wrote down all of it — every system that survived contact with a real ADHD brain.

**Wired Different** gives you 12 chapters of compatible software:

- Why willpower advice structurally fails you (and the 4 fuels that actually generate motivation)
- A second-brain setup that sticks past week two
- Making time visible, physical, and loud — the fix for time blindness
- How to start tasks when starting feels like climbing a wall
- Hyperfocus without the burnout crash
- Borrowing other people's executive function (body doubling, done right)
- ADHD-proof money automation that ends the invisible-bill tax
- Choosing careers that reward your wiring instead of punishing it
- Rejection sensitivity at work: pause protocols and word-for-word scripts
- Routines that survive the novelty cliff
- A fill-in template for your own one-page Personal Operating Manual

**This is for you if:**
- You're an adult with diagnosed or suspected ADHD and you're done with generic productivity advice
- You can hyperfocus for 6 hours but can't send one boring email
- Every system you've tried died between week two and week three

**Don't buy this if:**
- You want medical or medication advice — that belongs with your clinician, and this book says so on page one
- You're looking for a cure or "10x your life in 7 days" hype — there's zero toxic positivity in here
- You've never struggled with follow-through — this will read like a foreign language

12 chapters. ~28,000 words. PDF + EPUB. Read it in any order — the book is built for how your brain actually works.
